Readings and up to 1,000 log events are saved in this browser for each credential. Older events may have expired on the device. Firmware-dependent values appear only when reported; unconfirmed units and state codes are shown raw. Export contains readings and logs, without your encryption key.

How pairing works

1. Wake the device
Shake it until the lights pulse blue. Keep it within a few feet of this computer/phone.
2. Import & connect
Import your saved credential JSON once, then tap Connect JUUL and choose your device.
3. Ring & track
Ring your device, see live battery and pod usage, and track new puffs while connected.

Trouble? Bluetooth off, another app holding the connection, or an unsupported browser are the usual causes. Close the phone app, toggle Bluetooth, and retry. This page never sends your data anywhere — stats stay in this browser.

Independent clone built for personal device use. Not affiliated with JUUL Labs. Battery / pod / lock behaviour depends on what your unit exposes over GATT; the explorer and console show the ground truth for your hardware. Hit statistics are stored only in this browser’s local storage and can be reset or exported above.
